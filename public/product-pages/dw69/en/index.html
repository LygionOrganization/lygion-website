69mm modular drive wheel for robot chassis

A standard drive unit that combines wheel, motor, mount, and control interface

DW69 is designed for compact AGV, UGV, ROS rover, and education platforms. It integrates a 69mm wear-resistant rubber tire, 6061 aluminum wheel hub and base, and a geared stepper motor so teams can spend less time on mechanical sourcing and more time on chassis algorithms and upper-layer applications.

Why DW69

Easy to start with, flexible enough to extend

A conventional wheel setup still requires hub design, bearings, mounts, geared motors, drivers, and wiring. DW69 standardizes the repeated work inside chassis drive design so a working mobile platform can be assembled faster.

01

Stable low-speed drive for indoor mobile bases

The geared stepper setup and default firmware emphasize smooth, stable low-speed control for positioning, cruising, and lab experiments.

02

Load-bearing structure built into the wheel

An in-wheel flange bearing carries chassis load, reducing the pressure of designing extra external support structures.

03

Replaceable rubber tire surface

The 15mm contact width uses wear-resistant rubber. The tire surface can be replaced after wear without discarding the whole wheel assembly.

04

TTL bus control with cleaner wiring

The TTL bus stepper driver can share the same bus style as Feetech TTL servos, which is useful for multi-wheel and multi-actuator systems.

05

Heartbeat protection and soft start-stop

The driver supports heartbeat protection for safer behavior during communication loss. Soft start-stop reduces mechanical shock.

06

Open chassis references available

Two-wheel balance, sheet-metal 4WD, and aluminum-profile 4WD references can be reused to lower the cost from idea to first motion.

Product anatomy

One module handles four chassis drive jobs

  • Drive: geared stepper motor for mobile bases that need stable low-speed behavior.
  • Load: in-wheel flange bearing carries vehicle weight in a compact structure.
  • Mount: side M6 and front M4 mounting points; left and right sides are interchangeable.
  • Maintenance: replaceable rubber tire surface, with 6061 aluminum hub and base.
