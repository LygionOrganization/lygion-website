01

STRUCTURE

Let the mechanism carry more of the load

LinkArm-M takes a different mechanical approach from a typical serial arm. Its linkage and joint servos share the load, helping the arm stay stable while moving, starting, stopping, or holding position.

Better load distributionReduces long-duration, high-load demand on any single joint.
Greater overall rigidityArm posture stays more stable under load, during motion, and as the base accelerates or stops.
Built for longer tasksUnder the same workload, the servos are less likely to remain in a high-temperature state.

DECISION

When should you choose LinkArm-M?

Choose M when your robot already has a stable sub-controller and actuator architecture, and you only need LinkArm's mechanical structure and payload advantages.

LINKARM-LT

Choose the version with the ESP32-S3 sub-controller when:

  • The arm needs to operate as a standalone system
  • You want to add a dedicated arm sub-controller to the existing robot architecture
  • You want the arm controller to handle inverse kinematics and interpolation
