WHY THIS HUB

Built for compact robot joints

Small robots often need several encoders and servos in very limited space. The HC-1.25 8P Hub (A) keeps the communication protocol unchanged while distributing bus signals and power across eight HC-1.25-3P ports, making it a natural fit for dexterous hands, compact arms, and lightweight mechanisms.

WIRING

Simple connections, flexible power

Host sideConnect the HX-5264-3P port directly to a TTL Adapter (A) or Robot Driver for both data and power.
Device sideEight HC-1.25-3P ports support devices such as TTL Encoder E02, SC-0090-C043, SC-0090-C049, and compatible Feetech bus servos.
Isolated powerThe GH-1.25-3P port carries data only. Use it to connect a host or another hub while powering the devices separately.

DEVICE EXPANSION

Connect up to eight compact bus devices

One HC-1.25 8P Hub (A) can connect eight HC-1.25-3P bus devices. The host uses a single bus connection and addresses each encoder or micro servo individually by device ID.

Shared data busAll eight ports share the TTL communication line while retaining individual device IDs.
Compact harnessSmall connectors and lightweight cables suit dexterous hands, legged robots, and other space-sensitive designs.

DIMENSIONS

A slim board for narrow spaces

The board measures 21mm wide by 39mm long. Mounting-hole spacing is 15mm horizontally and 33mm vertically. Its narrow profile works well along robot joints and inside slim structural cavities.

COMPATIBLE DEVICES

Encoders and micro servos on one bus

Designed for HC1.25-3P TTL devices, including LYGION bus products and compatible Feetech STS, HLS, and SCS series servos.

BUS SYSTEM

Clean wiring for feedback and motion

The HC-1.25 8P Hub (A) brings multiple encoders and micro servos onto one TTL bus. The host reads or controls each device by ID, reducing the number of cables that must pass through small joints.

INTEGRATION NOTE

Small connectors need a considered power plan

HC-1.25 connectors are well suited to compact signal and lightweight wiring. Select the supply and cable capacity according to device count, cable length, current draw, and peak actuator load.
