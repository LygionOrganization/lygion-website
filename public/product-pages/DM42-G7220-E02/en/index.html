TTL BUS JOINT ACTUATOR

40 kg·cm Stepper Joint Actuator

DM42-G7220-E02

A compact actuator for robot joints, swing arms and rotary platforms. A geared stepper motor drives the output pulley through a secondary timing-belt reduction. A built-in 12-bit absolute magnetic encoder measures the output directly, while the paired TTL Stepper Driver (A) connects both the motor and encoder to the TTL bus.

PRODUCT BENEFITS

A compact joint module with integrated feedback

DM42-G7220-E02 combines mechanical reduction, a ready-to-use output pulley, absolute angle sensing and bus-based drive electronics in one joint-actuator package. It is intended for robotic mechanisms that need controlled low-to-medium-speed motion, continuous rotation or coordinated multi-axis movement, with a straightforward integration path.

PRODUCT STRUCTURE

Straightforward mechanical integration

M6 threaded holes on the main frame provide mounting options for different mechanisms. The output pulley includes eight M3 mounting holes, making it practical to attach a custom link, arm or rotary fixture.

MECHANICAL INTEGRATION

Mount it like a control servo

The 20 mm frame thickness is convenient for mounting to 20-series aluminum extrusion. The actuator is then controlled over the bus as a robot joint, with tutorials and examples available for initial setup.

System boundary: Although both connect through TTL Stepper Driver (A), the encoder and stepper driver remain separate bus devices. Each has its own device ID and can be configured using the supplied examples.

CONTROL & FEEDBACK

Position, speed and follower modes

TTL Stepper Driver (A) receives target positions or speeds over the bus and handles stepper motion locally. Follower mode is available for coordinated multi-axis mechanisms.

POSITION

Position mode

Controls the target angle of a joint, swing arm or rotary platform. The encoder provides the mechanism's current absolute output angle.

SPEED

Speed mode

Supports continuous rotation with configurable target speed and acceleration or deceleration for smoother starts and stops.

FOLLOWER

Follower mode

A follower device tracks a leader, supporting synchronized actuators, mirrored mechanisms and coupled axes.

12-bit absolute magnetic encoder at the output

A single revolution is represented by values from 0 to 4095. The current output position is available after power-up, and calibration data is retained without power. Output-angle feedback can be used to monitor joint state, detect lost steps, identify belt slip or check for mechanical deviation.

BUS INTEGRATION

Connect multiple joints on one bus

A PC or SBC such as Raspberry Pi or Jetson can connect through the USB port on TTL Adapter (A). ESP32, STM32 and Arduino controllers can use a supported single-wire TTL interface. Before adding multiple devices, assign a unique ID to every driver and encoder.

FD SOFTWARE

Bring-up without custom test code

FD software can discover devices, change IDs, display encoder angles, switch control modes and test motor movement. During initial setup, checking power, wiring and device status first helps separate mechanical or electrical issues from application-code problems.
