WHAT IT SOLVES

Why use an output-side absolute encoder?

TTL Encoder E02 gives the controller real mechanism feedback instead of only estimating from the motor side. It powers up with a known absolute angle, keeps calibration settings after power-off, and can share the same TTL bus with other robot devices.

01

Position Available at Startup

The single-turn absolute angle can be read immediately. This is useful for joints and steering modules that should recover state quickly without a homing routine.

No homing wait
02

Closer to the Load

Mount it near a timing pulley, gear, reducer output, or inside the joint to measure the angle after backlash, belt stretch, and transmission error.

Better reducer-side feedback
03

One Bus for Many Devices

Multiple encoders and other TTL bus devices can share one communication line, which keeps robot arms, grippers, and steering modules easier to wire and expand.

Control all bus devices through one link
04

Easy to Embed

The compact round board and HC1.25 connector fit small robot mechanisms where sensor space is limited.

Designed for embedded mounting

BOARD DETAIL

Main board resources

Magnetic Encoder ICDetects the rotation of the radial magnet. During installation, the magnet center should be aligned with the IC as closely as possible. A 6 x 2.5 mm radial magnet is included.
HC1.25-3P TTL Bus and Power PortConnect GND / VCC / Signal to the TTL bus. The included cable can connect one encoder directly, or multiple encoders can be connected through a Hub Board.

TTL BUS SYSTEM

Multiple encoders, multiple device types, one bus

Use TTL Adapter (A), a Hub Board, or a controller with single-wire TTL to place multiple encoders and other bus devices on the same communication link. After assigning each device a unique ID, a host or MCU can read and control them centrally.

MAGNET INSTALLATION

Installation notes

TTL Encoder E02 measures angle by sensing the rotation of a radial magnet. For reliable readings, align the magnet rotation axis with the center of the encoder IC and keep the magnet-to-IC distance around 0.8-1.5 mm. A stable mechanical mount gives more stable feedback.

ConcentricityKeep the magnet rotation axis as close as possible to the center of the encoder IC.
GapDesign for about 1 mm spacing between the magnet and IC; 0.8-1.5 mm is usable.
FixingSecure both the magnet and the encoder board to avoid vibration or off-axis rotation causing unstable readings.
CalibrationAfter assembly, set the current position as the reference angle. Calibration parameters are retained after power-off.
